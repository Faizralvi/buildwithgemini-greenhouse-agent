# My agent: greenhouse-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts discover, care for, and purchase indoor plants with a catalog of house plants and botanical care supplies.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User lighting conditions, plant experience level, watering reminders, and owned plant collection
- Tools: Search plant inventory, get plant care details, add new plants, and update stock/care logs in Firestore
- Catalog/UI: Collection of indoor plants with name, species, care level, light needs, watering schedule, price, and stock
- Image gen: Botanical visualization and healthy plant preview images
- Sandbox: Calculating custom watering intervals and soil mixture ratios

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for soil / watering calculations, Firestore for inventory
